# Phase 5.2.2 — Synthesis: Unified Campaigns-Tab Action List

## Method

This document merges Claude Opus 4.7's deep campaigns-tab audit (36 findings: 5 P0, 9 P1, 14 P2, 8 P3) with codex/GPT-5's independent peer review (4 new findings + verdicts on Opus's 36). The merge rules are mechanical: **AGREE** findings keep Opus's severity; **PARTIAL** findings adopt codex's adjusted severity (codex was more rigorous about distinguishing "demonstrated production-path corruption" from "defensive hardening that would catch a future regression"); **DISAGREE** findings are dropped entirely (false positives where Opus over-reached or misread the contract); **EXTEND** findings combine Opus's identification with codex's broader scope; **NEW** findings are added at codex's chosen severity.

Opus has a clear bias toward classifying anything that touches data integrity as P0 — five of Opus's P0 claims were downgraded by codex (1 dropped, 4 demoted to P1/P2/P3) because the alleged corruption either matched the documented cross-channel contract (P0-05) or was already gated by upstream guards (P0-04, P0-01 lossy-ID claim). The two true silent-data-corruption defects that survive are AUDIT-P0-02 (`isOrganicSource` over-permissive for `other-paid`/empty) and AUDIT-P0-03 (Organic refund-day drop). The biggest signal codex added: **all four codex new findings are namespace-drop defects in drilldown state** — the campaign drawer drops `storeId` from drilldown keys, the CPM previous-period series drops `storeId`+`platform`, the AdsDrawer drops `platform`, and `AttributionAnalysisPanel` doesn't clamp signed revenue widths. CODEX-NEW-P1-01 (campaign drilldown drops `storeId` in all-stores view) is the highest-impact item in this batch — it can silently merge two stores' data into one drawer when they share a campaign ID.

## Severity Legend

- **P0** — Silent data corruption (wrong number displayed as truth, no error)
- **P1** — Wrong math / contract mismatch (loud failure OR contradicts ROADMAP spec)
- **P2** — Missing edge case or defensive gap (works on happy path, fails on unusual input)
- **P3** — Test coverage gap (algorithm OK but no test pins behavior)
